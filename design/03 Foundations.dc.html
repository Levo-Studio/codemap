Elevation

Four levels. The map sits flat; only things that float over the map get a shadow.

0 · CanvasMap background, dot grid
1 · SurfaceNodes, containers, panel. Border only
2 · FloatingChat bar, banners, zoom control
3 · OverlayCommand palette, onboarding. With scrim

Motion

Calm and informative. Motion only signals that something is happening in the code; it never decorates. No bounce, no overshoot. With “Reduce motion” every loop becomes a static marker.
